# Design — Marek & Paulo

Two parts. **Current state** describes the site as it is on `main`
(14 November 2026), measured from `css/style.css`, the pages and
`tools/make_pages.py`. **Target direction** is where the redesign is
heading, and **What's not working yet** lists the gaps between the two.
When a change contradicts the current state, update this document in the
same commit; when it moves the site towards the target, tick it off the gap
list.

---

# Current state

## The idea in one sentence

A hand-painted invitation to an Algarve summer: a watercolour street of
whitewashed Tavira houses you can walk into, and behind each door a page that
reads like the printed invitation — handwritten titles in one ink per page,
small-caps labels, a bookish serif, and a watercolour object beside the text.

### Page inks (`--topic`)

One ink per page, every section on that page uses it. Small text uses
`--ink` (the ink mixed 20 % towards `--text`, passes AA); at night the ink is
mixed 50 % towards white.

| Name | Hex | Pages |
|---|---|---|

| TERRACOTTA | `#b5432a` | Program, Weather — the roofs |

### The strip

The home page link band is a fixed brick, outside the token system:
`#a84f36` with cream `#fbf3e4` text (5 : 1); at night `#55302a` /
`#e6e0d0`.

### Night

A full second palette, not an inversion: deep indigo, the painting swaps to
its night layer, windows and lamp glow amber (`mix-blend-mode: screen`), the
sun becomes a moon. Toggle in the nav, saved in `localStorage` (`theme`),
defaults to the OS setting. Paper objects (travel map, polaroids) stay paper,
only dimmed.

**Count:** 7 base tokens × 2 themes, 3 house colours, 3 inks, 2 strip
colours, plus amber glow — about 16 distinct colours in use.

## Typography

Five families, one job each, loaded on every page from Google Fonts in one
request (Beth Ellen; Cormorant Garamond 500/600; Cormorant SC 700; Inter
400/500/600; Lora 400/600/400 italic).

| Token | Family | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--serif` | Lora | **Everything you read**: body, facts, figures |
| `--sc` | Cormorant SC 700 | **Every label**: line above/below a title, `<dt>`, categories, the hero date line. Never tracked-out uppercase. |
| `--script` | Beth Ellen | **Titles**: h1 48–76 px, h2 40–58 px |
| `--sans` | Inter | **Controls and fine print**: nav, buttons, chips, the strip, footer links, map UI, credits |
| `--display` | Cormorant Garamond | Brand name in the nav and the hero title only |

Scale around 17 px body: `--fs-xs` 12 · `--fs-s` 14 · `--fs-m` 15 ·
`--fs-base` 17 · `--fs-label` 16 · `--fs-l` 20 · `--fs-xl` 24 ·
`--fs-xxl` 28. Display sizes use `clamp()`.

Hero: h1 `clamp(26px, 50u, 56px)`, `.14em` tracking; tagline "To this and
every summer"; date line "1 – 3 October 2027 · Tavira, Algarve" in small
caps. `u` is one pixel of the 1926 px painting, so the title scales with it.

Title case is mixed: mostly lowercase ("the Program", "good to know",
"the guests") with exceptions ("Travel", "What to Pack", "the Wedding Day").

## Layout and spacing

- **Nav**: fixed, 60 px; transparent over the home hero, frosted
  (`blur(12px)`, 82 % `--bg`, hairline) once scrolled and always on inner
  pages. Brand left; pill links (Inter 14 px, `6px 12px`); RSVP a dark pill;
  a 38 px day/night button. Below 820 px a hamburger opens a card menu with
  16 px links and 12 px padding.
- **Home hero**: the painting is 1926 × 817 px plus a 360 px sky band where
  the names sit. Hotspots are placed in picture pixels (`--x/--y/--w/--h`).
  Width fills the screen but keeps the doors above the fold; under 860 px the
  street stays 860 px wide and scrolls sideways ("Swipe to see the whole
  street").
- **Home below the village**: one muted hint line ("Everything in the village
  is clickable…"), then the brick **strip** across the full width: six links
  (Program, Stay, Travel, Weather, Algarve, Guests — no RSVP), icon over an
  Inter label, 6 across, 3 × 2 on phones. Then the story section (hidden
  until `data-storymap` has a link) and a one-line footer.
- **Inner pages**: `.page.inv`, 1000 px max, 108 px top padding, 20 px side
  gutter. Text left, watercolour right (`.inv-grid`,
  `1fr / minmax(200px, 320px)`); sections separated by a hairline and
  48–80 px. One column under 760 px; watercolours shrink to 150 px accents.
- **Footer** (inner pages and Guests): a hairline, then one row: "‹ Previous"
  (Inter 14 px, muted), the village painting small (220–320 px, day/night
  copies from `tools/village_mini.py`) leading home, "Next ›"; the names/date
  line in 12 px below.
- **Radii**: 16 px cards, maps, menu · 14 px highlights, list items ·
  10–12 px small tiles, menu links · 999 px pills, chips · 3 px polaroids.
- **Shadow**: one soft shadow (`0 1px 2px / 0 8px 24px`, 4–8 % black) on
  hover and floating UI.
- **Spacing**: fluid `clamp()` throughout, no fixed spacing scale. Content is
  left-aligned on inner pages, centred on Home.
- **Breakpoints**: 860 (hero), 820 (nav), 760 (one column), 560 (lists).

## Motion

- Day ↔ night: 1.2 s cross-fade of colours and painting layers; windows light
  0.6 s later; the lamp flickers on.
- Hotspots: doors swing open in 3D, the plane speeds up, the weathervane
  spins (press and hold: points to you), the tile lifts, the orange tree drops
  oranges.
- Ambient: the plane drifts (6 s loop), the vane sways (7 s loop).
- Page transitions: entering from the village zooms 3.2× into the door and
  fades to paper; the footer village fades out and Home opens at 2.4× and
  zooms out.
- Story section unfolds with scroll.
- Easter eggs (`js/eggs.js`): disco ball with music and lights, crabs, the
  sardine and a school of sardines, party mode with bunting (`?party`).
- Small UI: 0.2–0.3 s hovers, menu slide, mini-village lift.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` turns all of it off.

## Tone of voice

Warm, personal, first person plural, a little playful ("pick a driver and let
them be the hero"). English. Script titles mostly lowercase. Small-caps lines
sometimes run into the title ("Let us help you plan your" → *Travel*).

## Practical notes

- Inner pages: edit `tools/make_pages.py` and re-run, don't edit the HTML.
- CSS is one file, `css/style.css` (~1450 lines), cache-busted with `?v=`.
- Painting coordinates are in picture pixels (1926 × 817); see the top of
  `style.css`.
- Maps: ArcGIS Maps SDK 5.2 + Calcite, loaded as ES modules via
  `js/arcgis-boot.js`, only on Stay and Algarve, started when the map
  scrolls into view. Guests embeds a separate map site in an `iframe`.

# Target direction

**Modern and minimalist, with an artsy character.** The site should feel
like a quiet, well-made printed piece, not a theme. The artsy part comes from
**one or two deliberate details**: the watercolour village and the
handwritten title are the natural candidates. Everything around them is
plain.

**Niche and clean, not trendy-template.** No stock wedding tropes, no
effects for their own sake. Restraint over effects; **if in doubt, remove
rather than add.**

**Principles**

1. **Generous whitespace.** Fewer elements per screen, more air between
   them. One idea per section.
2. **Tight palette.** Paper, ink (text), one muted tone, and **one** accent
   ink for the whole site. Night is the same palette darkened, not a second
   world. Pigments from the painting stay in the painting.
3. **Max two typefaces.** A serif for reading and headings (with small caps
   for labels from the same family if it has them), plus either the script
   *as the one artsy detail* or a sans for controls — not both.
4. **Little text.** Guests should find within seconds: **date, place,
   schedule, RSVP, travel/accommodation.** Everything else is collapsed
   (`<details>`) or cut. A section is a heading, a few facts, at most two
   sentences.
5. **UX first.**
   - Clear navigation with few items; the essentials are reachable from
     Home in one tap.
   - An obvious RSVP path: visible on Home, in the nav, and at the end of the
     key pages, and it works.
   - Mobile-first: designed at 375 px, enhanced upwards. No sideways scrolling
     needed to reach content.
   - Fast: the first screen loads with little more than the HTML, CSS, one
     image and the fonts. Heavy things (maps, easter eggs, night layers) load
     only when asked for.
   - Accessible: WCAG AA contrast for all text, 44 × 44 px tap targets, visible
     focus, real headings and labels.
6. **Motion only where it helps** — to show where you came from or that
   something responded. Subtle and short (≤ 300 ms for UI; one longer
   transition at most), always off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

# What's not working yet

Where the current site falls short of the target, roughly by impact:

1. **No working RSVP.** The RSVP page is a placeholder ("Reply form to
   follow."). On Home, RSVP is only behind a door in the painting and in the
   nav — the brick strip lists six pages but not RSVP.
2. **Essentials aren't at a glance.** Date and place are a small-caps line on
   Home; the schedule says "Details of the day to follow"; there is no single
   place with date · place · schedule · RSVP · how to get there.
3. **Five typefaces** (Lora, Cormorant SC, Beth Ellen, Inter, Cormorant
   Garamond) against a target of two; that's also five font downloads on
   every page.
4. **Too many colours.** ~16 in use: three house colours, three page inks,
   a separate brick for the strip, olive logo ink, plus a full second night
   palette. The target is paper, ink, muted, one accent.
5. **Artsy details everywhere instead of one or two.** Interactive
   painting, script titles, a watercolour object per section, a torn paper
   map, polaroid ring, mini-village footer, easter eggs, party mode,
   day/night theme — each is charming, together they compete.
6. **Home is navigation by exploration.** "Everything in the village is
   clickable" asks guests to discover links; on phones the street is 860 px
   wide and must be swiped.
7. **Too much motion.** Ambient loops (plane, vane), 3D doors, 3.2× zoom page
   transitions, lamp flicker and easter eggs. Reduced-motion is respected,
   but the default is far from "subtle and short".
8. **Text-heavy secondary pages.** Stay (~270 words), Algarve (~275) and
   Travel (~210) carry most of the copy; "our favourites", "dishes to try",
   "at the Quinta" and the carousel are secondary and not collapsed.
9. **Tap targets below 44 px.** Desktop nav pills ~31 px tall, the theme
   button 38 px, footer ‹ › links ~32 px.
10. **Weight.** The home painting is ~520 KB in two layers plus PNG overlays;
    the ArcGIS SDK + Calcite on Stay and Algarve are heavy (lazy, but still
    loaded just by scrolling past), and the five font families load on every page.
11. **Naming and casing are inconsistent.** The nav says *Stay*, the page
    says *the Hotel*; titles mix "the guests", "Travel", "What to Pack";
    small-caps lines split sentences across the title.
12. **No spacing scale.** Spacing is ad-hoc `clamp()` values and radii come in
    five sizes; a small fixed scale would keep things calm.
