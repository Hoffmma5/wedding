# Current state

## The idea in one sentence

A hand-painted invitation to an Algarve summer: a watercolour street of
whitewashed Tavira houses you can walk into, and behind each door a page that
reads like the printed invitation — handwritten titles in one ink per page,
small-caps labels, a bookish serif, and a watercolour object beside the text.

### The strip

The home page strip and every footer are terracotta `#B66238` (the title
colour) by day, with white text (4.4 : 1, just under AA for small text).
The hero names, the favicon and the Program / Weather page ink use the
same `#B66238`. At night the terracotta changes the way it would after
sunset: the names turn a warm peach `#F0B48C` (4.9 : 1 on the sky), the
strip and footers a dusk terracotta `#7D4128` with cream `#F7EBDC` text,
and the favicon a lighter terracotta on dark tabs.

### Night

A full second palette, not an inversion: deep indigo, the painting swaps to
its night layer, windows and lamp glow amber (`mix-blend-mode: screen`), the
the painted signs dim. Toggle in the nav, saved in `localStorage` (`theme`),
defaults to the OS setting. Paper objects (the travel map) stay paper,
only dimmed.

**Count:** 7 base tokens × 2 themes, 3 house colours, 3 inks, 2 strip
colours, plus amber glow — about 16 distinct colours in use.

## Typography

Three families, one job each, loaded on every page from Google Fonts in one
request (Beth Ellen; Cormorant SC 700; Lora 400/600/400 italic — five
styles, down from five families and ten styles).

| Token | Family | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--serif` | Lora | **Everything you read**: body, facts, figures — and the controls |
| `--sc` | Cormorant SC 700 | **Every label**: line above/below a title, `<dt>`, categories, the hero date line. Never tracked-out uppercase. Also the brand name in the nav and the hero title, in uppercase with wide tracking. |
| `--script` | Beth Ellen | **Titles**: h1 48–76 px, h2 40–58 px; on phones (≤ 560 px) 44 / 36 px |
| `--sans` | = `--serif` | **Controls and fine print**: nav, buttons, chips, the strip, footer links, map UI, credits. Kept as a token so controls could get their own face again in one line. |

Scale around 17 px body: `--fs-xs` 12 · `--fs-s` 14 · `--fs-m` 15 ·
`--fs-base` 17 · `--fs-label` 16 · `--fs-l` 20 · `--fs-xl` 24 ·
`--fs-xxl` 28. Display sizes use `clamp()`.

Hero: h1 `clamp(26px, 50u, 56px)`, `.14em` tracking; tagline "To this and
every summer"; date line "1 – 3 October 2027 · Tavira, Algarve" in small
caps. `u` is one pixel of the 1926 px painting, so the title scales with it.

Title case is mixed: mostly lowercase ("the Program", "good to know",
"the guests") with exceptions ("Travel", "What to Pack", "the Wedding Day").

  pages. Brand left; pill links (Lora 14 px, 44 px tall); RSVP a dark pill;

  960 px a hamburger opens a card menu with 16 px links, 44 px tall, and

- **Home hero**: the painting is 1926 × 817 px plus a 360 px sky band where
  the names sit. Hotspots are placed in picture pixels (`--x/--y/--w/--h`).
  Width fills the screen but keeps the doors above the fold; under 860 px the
  street stays 860 px wide and scrolls sideways ("Swipe to see the whole
  street").
- **Home below the village**: one muted italic line in the white, "Our
  village is interactive – open the doors, switch on the lamp, shake the
  orange tree… play around." Then the terracotta **strip** across the full
  width, taller: *The weekend at a glance* in small caps, three columns —
  When (1 – 3 October 2027), Where (Tavira, Faro, Portugal), Getting there
  (Fly to Faro), each with an ink-and-wash icon (calendar, pin, suitcase) —
  then a hairline and the six page links (Program, Stay, Travel, Weather,
  Algarve, Guests), icon over a Lora label, 6 across, 3 × 2 on phones.
  `tools/make_pages.py` writes the whole strip between `<!-- strip -->`
  markers. Then the story section (hidden until `data-storymap` has a link)
  and a one-line footer. The hero has the names and tagline only — no date
  line.
- **Doors**: the blue house leads to Program, the yellow house to
  Accommodation, the coral house to RSVP.
- **Title order**: the script title first, its small-caps subheading
  always below it (never above), in the title's colour, with room for the
  script's long descenders (1 em under an h1, .75 em under an h2).
- **Footers**: terracotta `#B66238`, full width, white text; on Home the
  strip runs straight into it.
- **Centred design**: page and section titles sit in the middle; a title
  that shares a row with a picture keeps to its column (Program's Friday
  and Sunday). Text that stands alone under a centred title is centred too
  (`.prose.center`, 600 px).
- **Inner pages**: `.page.inv`, 1000 px max, 108 px top padding, 20 px side
  gutter. The page title is centred on top; under it, text and a
  watercolour side by side (`.inv-grid`, `1fr / minmax(200px, 320px)`).
  Every other row is mirrored (`.flip`: the picture on the left) —
  Weather's sun, Program's Sunday oysters and the Algarve's sardine tile.
  Rows with a small picture set `--side` (the column is exactly as wide as
  the picture) and sit as a centred group, 760 px max: the sun (200 px)
  and the crabs (240 px). Travel and Weather: the title is centred within the text column and the
  picture sits beside the whole column — Travel's torn map is exactly as tall
  as title + facts, Weather's sun (180–280 px) is on the left; the shrimp is a small tilted vignette above the
  centred *transfer* section. Sections are separated by a hairline and
  48–80 px. One column under 760 px, always text first and the picture
  after it; watercolours shrink to 150 px accents.
- **Footer** (inner pages and Guests): a hairline, then one row: "‹ Previous"
  (Lora 14 px, 44 px tall), the logo orange in white (40 px) leading home, "Next ›"; the
  names/date line in 12 px below.
- **Radii**: 16 px cards, maps, menu · 14 px highlights, list items ·
  10–12 px small tiles, menu links · 999 px pills, chips · 14 px photo mosaic
  (10 px on phones).
- **Shadow**: one soft shadow (`0 1px 2px / 0 8px 24px`, 4–8 % black) on
  hover and floating UI.
- **Spacing**: fluid `clamp()` throughout, no fixed spacing scale. Content is
  left-aligned on inner pages, centred on Home.
- **Breakpoints**: 960 (nav), 860 (hero), 760 (one column), 560 (lists,

- **Touch**: every control is at least 44 × 44 px. Inline links inside
  running text are exempt.

  0.6 s later; the lamp flickers on.
- Hotspots: doors swing open in 3D, the weathervane spins (press and hold:
  points to you), the Algarve tile lifts, the Faro sign tilts on its

# Target direction

**Modern and minimalist, with an artsy character.** The site should feel
like a quiet, well-made printed piece, not a theme. The artsy part comes from
**one or two deliberate details**: the watercolour village and the
handwritten title are the natural candidates. Everything around them is
plain.

**Niche and clean, not trendy-template.** No stock wedding tropes, no
effects for their own sake. Restraint over effects; **if in doubt, remove
rather than add.**

**Principles**

1. **Generous whitespace.** Fewer elements per screen, more air between
   them. One idea per section.
2. **Tight palette.** Paper, ink (text), one muted tone, and **one** accent
   ink for the whole site. Night is the same palette darkened, not a second
   world. Pigments from the painting stay in the painting.
3. **Max two typefaces.** A serif for reading and headings (with small caps
   for labels from the same family if it has them), plus either the script
   *as the one artsy detail* or a sans for controls — not both.
4. **Little text.** Guests should find within seconds: **date, place,
   schedule, RSVP, travel/accommodation.** Everything else is collapsed
   (`<details>`) or cut. A section is a heading, a few facts, at most two
   sentences.
5. **UX first.**
   - Clear navigation with few items; the essentials are reachable from
     Home in one tap.
   - An obvious RSVP path: visible on Home, in the nav, and at the end of the
     key pages, and it works.
   - Mobile-first: designed at 375 px, enhanced upwards. No sideways scrolling
     needed to reach content.
   - Fast: the first screen loads with little more than the HTML, CSS, one
     image and the fonts. Heavy things (maps, easter eggs, night layers) load
     only when asked for.
   - Accessible: WCAG AA contrast for all text, 44 × 44 px tap targets, visible
     focus, real headings and labels.
6. **Motion only where it helps** — to show where you came from or that
   something responded. Subtle and short (≤ 300 ms for UI; one longer
   transition at most), always off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

# What's not working yet

Where the current site falls short of the target, roughly by impact:

1. **No working RSVP form.** RSVP is the dark pill in the header on every
   page and the coral door on Home — deliberately nowhere else. Still open: the RSVP page
   itself is a placeholder ("Reply form to follow."); it needs a form and
   somewhere for the answers to go.
2. ~~**Essentials aren't at a glance.**~~ Done: *at a glance* on Home says
   when and where; schedule, stay and travel are one tap away on the strip,
   RSVP in the header.
3. **Three typefaces** (Lora, Cormorant SC, Beth Ellen) against a target of
   two — down from five; Inter and Cormorant Garamond are gone. Reaching two
   means either labels in Lora small caps (synthesised, so weaker) or
   dropping the script, which is the artsy detail; three is a deliberate
   stop.
4. **Too many colours.** ~16 in use: three house colours, three page inks,
   a separate brick for the strip, olive logo ink, plus a full second night
   palette. The target is paper, ink, muted, one accent.
5. **Artsy details everywhere instead of one or two.** Interactive
   painting, script titles, a watercolour object per section, a torn paper
   map, easter eggs, party mode, day/night theme — each is charming,
   together they compete. Fewer than before: the footer is the logo orange,
   the polaroid rings are a plain photo mosaic, the sky's sun and plane are
   gone (Weather is the weathervane, Travel a painted Faro sign), and the icons
   share the painting's ink-and-wash language instead of adding a second,
   UI-icon style.
6. **Home is navigation by exploration.** "Everything in the village is
   clickable" asks guests to discover links; on phones the street is 860 px
   wide and must be swiped.
7. **Too much motion.** The vane's ambient loop, 3D doors, lamp flicker and
   easter eggs (the plane is gone, the door zoom is now 2× in 0.5 s). Reduced-motion is respected,
   but the default is far from "subtle and short".
8. **Secondary pages, partly trimmed.** Intros are short, *dishes to try*
   is folded and Stay shows five photos in one mosaic, the other twelve
   behind "See all".
   The Algarve intro stays three short paragraphs on purpose — it is the
   personal part. Still open: *our favourites* (the map app) is not folded.
9. ~~**Tap targets below 44 px.**~~ Done: all controls are 44 px.
10. **Weight.** The home painting is ~520 KB in two layers plus PNG overlays;
    the ArcGIS SDK + Calcite on Stay and Algarve are heavy (lazy, but still
    loaded just by scrolling past); three font families load on every page.
11. **Naming and casing are inconsistent.** The nav says *Stay*, the page
    says *the Hotel*; titles mix "the guests", "Travel", "What to Pack";
    small-caps lines split sentences across the title. On Home the door
    tooltip says "Accommodation" while the strip says "Stay".
12. **No spacing scale.** Spacing is ad-hoc `clamp()` values and radii come in
    five sizes; a small fixed scale would keep things calm.
