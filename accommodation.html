Something for everyone

at the Quinta

The Quinta is a little world of its own, up in the hills with a view all the way to the sea. Between the celebrations there’s plenty to keep everyone happy.

  • For the little onesA playground, the animal farm (say hello to the donkey and the peacocks) and the pool for endless splashing.
  • For the grown-upsThe pool and the pool bar, a little spa with a sauna, a gym with a view – and plenty of shady corners for doing absolutely nothing.

the Bedrooms

Where will we sleep?

Everyone stays with us at Hotel Quinta do Marco for both nights. We’ll let each of you know personally which room is yours.

The rooms are bright and comfortable, and each one has its own balcony or terrace looking out over the hills – perfect for a slow morning coffee after a long night.
